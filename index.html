This Privacy Policy explains how Kawizend, the publishing name of Sinan Aghayev (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Askance (the “App”). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: Askance does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or contact details. The App saves your game progress and settings on your own device. To show ads and to process in-app purchases, we use Unity, Google, and Apple, which may collect device identifiers and similar technical data as described below.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device so the game works and remembers your progress. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your level progress, completed levels, and current level.
  • Game preferences and settings (for example music, sound effects, haptics, danger-highlight, and language).
  • In-game item balances such as hints, and counters used to schedule ads.
  • A record of in-app purchases you have made, so purchased features stay unlocked.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the App’s data (Android) or by uninstalling the App from your device.

Information collected automatically by third parties

We do not build our own profile of you, but the third-party services we use to run the App (advertising and payments) may automatically collect technical information. Depending on the service and your device settings, this can include:

  • Device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android, or Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS — only if you allow tracking when asked), and other device or app identifiers (such as Apple’s Identifier for Vendors).
  • Device and system information (such as device model, operating-system version, language, and region).
  • IP address and approximate, coarse location derived from it.
  • Ad-interaction data (for example whether an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked) used for delivering, capping, and measuring ads.
  • Purchase transaction details needed to complete and confirm in-app purchases.

We do not collect or have access to your payment-card number, bank details, or similar financial account information — those are handled directly by Google or Apple.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and improve the App, specifically to:

  • Save and restore your game progress and settings.
  • Display advertising, including interstitial and rewarded ads, and control how often ads appear.
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and keep purchased content unlocked.
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the App and prevent abuse.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies. Unity acts as an advertising partner and may collect and use device identifiers and the technical information described above to select, deliver, cap, and measure ads, including rewarded ads that let you continue a game. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized.

You can limit ad personalization at the device level by resetting or deleting your advertising identifier, or by enabling “Opt out of Ads Personalization” in your device’s settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising). To learn how Unity handles this data, see the Unity Privacy Policy and Unity’s Player Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (for example, unlimited hints) processed through Google Play Billing on Android or Apple In-App Purchase on iOS. When you make a purchase, Google or Apple processes the payment and shares a confirmation and transaction details with the App so your purchase can be delivered. We never receive your full payment-card or bank-account information. Their handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as follows:

  • With Unity Technologies, to provide advertising within the App.
  • With Google (Google Play), to distribute the App and process in-app purchases on Android.
  • With Apple (App Store), to distribute the App and process in-app purchases on iOS.
  • Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or Kawizend.

7. Data Retention

Information stored on your device remains until you clear the App’s data (Android) or uninstall the App. Information collected by our third-party partners is retained by them in accordance with their own privacy policies and retention practices.

8. Children’s Privacy

Askance is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate steps to address it. Where the App is made available to a mixed audience, our advertising partners are expected to handle ad serving in a manner consistent with applicable children’s-privacy requirements and the policies of the app store you downloaded the App from.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not maintain accounts or collect your contact details, most such requests are best directed to the relevant third-party service (Unity, Google, or Apple). You can also:

  • Reset or delete your device advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Clear the App’s locally stored data (Android), or uninstall the App, to remove on-device information.
  • Contact us with any privacy question or request using the details below.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Users

The App is available in multiple regions. Our third-party partners may process and store information in countries other than your own, whose data-protection laws may differ from those of your country. By using the App, you understand that information may be processed as described in this policy and in our partners’ policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be communicated through the App or its app store listing. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
