Privacy Policy

Askance

How this app handles your information.

PublisherSinan Aghayev (App Store) · Kawizend (Google Play)
Effective dateSeptember 8, 2026
Last updatedOctober 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Askance (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Askance (the “App”). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. Askance is developed by Sinan Aghayev and is published under his own name on the Apple App Store and under the developer name Kawizend on Google Play. Both refer to the same developer, and this policy applies to both versions of the App.

In short: Askance does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or contact details. The App saves your game progress and settings on your own device. We use a small number of third-party services: Unity to show ads, Google Firebase to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, Meta to measure how well our ads for Askance work, and the app store you downloaded the App from (Apple or Google) for purchases. These services may collect device identifiers, gameplay events and similar technical data, as described below. On iPhone and iPad, we ask for your permission before your device’s advertising identifier is used for tracking.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device so the game works and remembers your progress. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your level progress, completed levels, and current level.
  • Game preferences and settings (for example music, sound effects, haptics, danger-highlight, and language).
  • In-game item balances such as hints, and counters used to schedule ads.
  • A record of in-app purchases you have made, so purchased features stay unlocked.

You can clear this information at any time by using the in-game reset option (where available), or by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App from your device.

Information collected automatically by third-party services

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not receive your name or contact details. The third-party services we use to run the App may automatically collect technical information. Depending on the service and your device settings, this can include:

  • Identifiers: device advertising identifiers (the Google Advertising ID on Android, or Apple’s IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking), and app-instance or installation identifiers generated by the services themselves.
  • Device and system information: such as device model, operating-system version, language, region, and app version.
  • Approximate location: an IP address and the coarse location (such as country or city) derived from it. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Gameplay events: for example when you start, complete, fail, restart or leave a level; the level number; how long it took; how many attempts and hints were used; whether you finished or skipped the tutorial; results of Shrouded-mode runs; and when an ad is shown.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: if the App crashes or hits an error, technical details such as the error stack trace, device state at that moment, and the level being played.
  • Ad-interaction data: for example whether an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked, used to deliver, cap, and measure ads.
  • Purchase information: the item purchased, its price and currency, and the transaction details needed to complete and confirm an in-app purchase.

We do not collect or have access to your payment-card number, bank details, or similar financial account information — those are handled directly by Apple or Google.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and improve the App, specifically to:

  • Save and restore your game progress and settings.
  • Understand, in aggregate, how the game is played — for example which levels players find too hard or where they stop playing — so we can improve level design and difficulty.
  • Detect, diagnose and fix crashes and bugs.
  • Display advertising, including interstitial and rewarded ads, and control how often ads appear.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our own advertising campaigns for Askance (for example, whether an ad on Facebook or Instagram led to an install, and whether those players went on to play or make a purchase).
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and keep purchased content unlocked.
  • Show achievements and leaderboards if you use Game Center on iPhone or iPad.
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the App and prevent abuse.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. Firebase Analytics receives the gameplay events, identifiers, device information and approximate location described above so we can see aggregated statistics about how the game is played. Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports and diagnostic information so we can fix problems. We use these reports to improve the game; we do not use them to identify you personally. To learn how Google handles this data, see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Firebase privacy information.

4. Advertising

Ads shown in the App

The App displays advertisements through Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies. Unity acts as an advertising partner and may collect and use device identifiers and the technical information described above to select, deliver, cap, and measure ads, including rewarded ads that let you continue a game. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized. To learn how Unity handles this data, see the Unity Privacy Policy and Unity’s Player Privacy Policy.

Measuring our own ads (Meta)

We advertise Askance on Meta’s platforms (such as Facebook and Instagram). The App includes the Meta SDK (Facebook SDK) so that Meta can tell us how well those ads perform. The App sends Meta a limited set of events: that the App was opened, that you completed the tutorial, that you reached certain level milestones, and in-app purchases (item, price and currency), together with your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS, only if you allow tracking) and the device information described above. Meta uses this to attribute installs to our ads, to report results to us, and to help our ads reach people who are likely to enjoy the game. The App does not use Facebook Login and does not access your Facebook account, friends, or posts. To learn more, see the Meta Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission on iPhone and iPad

On iOS, the App asks for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” the IDFA is not shared. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your ad choices

You can limit ad personalization and measurement at the device level by resetting or deleting your advertising identifier, or by enabling “Opt out of Ads Personalization” in your device’s settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising). You can also manage how Meta uses information from apps in your Facebook or Instagram settings under “Your activity off Meta technologies.”

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (for example, unlimited hints, hint packs, or removing ads) processed through the Apple App Store (on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Billing (on Android). When you make a purchase, Apple or Google processes the payment and shares a confirmation and transaction details with the App so your purchase can be delivered. The item, price and currency of a purchase are also recorded by Firebase Analytics and sent to Meta as described above. We never receive your full payment-card or bank-account information. Apple’s and Google’s handling of your data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Game Center and Ratings

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the App reports your campaign progress, your best Shrouded-mode score and the achievements you unlock to Game Center so they can appear in leaderboards and achievements. This is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings and the Apple Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple ID. From time to time the App may also show the store’s built-in rating prompt (Apple or Google). Any rating or review you leave is handled by the store, not by us.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only as follows:

  • With Google (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics), to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.
  • With Meta Platforms, to measure and improve our advertising campaigns for the App.
  • With Unity Technologies, to provide advertising within the App.
  • With Apple (App Store, Game Center) and Google (Google Play), to distribute the App, process in-app purchases and, on iOS, provide Game Center features.
  • Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or ourselves.

Under some laws (for example, certain U.S. state privacy laws), providing device identifiers and app activity to advertising partners such as Meta and Unity may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising or “targeted advertising.” You can opt out of this by using the advertising-identifier and tracking settings described in Section 4, or by contacting us.

9. Legal Bases for Processing

Where data-protection laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to deliver in-app purchases you make.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the App working, fix crashes, understand in aggregate how the game is played, and keep the App secure.
  • Consent — where the law requires it for the use of advertising identifiers for advertising and ad measurement (for example, the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS). You can withdraw consent at any time through your device settings as described in Section 4.

For the purposes of these laws, the data controller is Sinan Aghayev, who can be reached at the contact details below.

10. Data Retention

Information stored on your device remains until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. User-level analytics data in Firebase is kept for no longer than 14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain. Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are kept for up to 90 days. Information collected by Meta, Unity, Apple and Google is retained by them in accordance with their own privacy policies and retention practices.

11. Children’s Privacy

Askance is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

12. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, Türkiye, or certain U.S. states), you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Because we do not maintain accounts or collect your contact details, we usually cannot link analytics or advertising data to you personally. You can:

  • Reset or delete your device advertising identifier, opt out of personalized ads, or deny tracking on iOS, in your device settings.
  • Clear the App’s data or uninstall the App, which removes on-device information and causes the App’s analytics identifiers to be regenerated.
  • Manage information Meta receives from apps in your Facebook or Instagram settings (“Your activity off Meta technologies”).
  • Contact us with any privacy question or request using the details below. We will respond within the time required by applicable law and will help pass your request to the relevant service where we can.

Our third-party partners, including Google, Meta and Unity, may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States, whose data-protection laws may differ from those of your country. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses provided by those partners.
